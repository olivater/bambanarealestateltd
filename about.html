About Bambana

Making home search feel personal again — with the warmth of a Ugandan family and the standards of global luxury.

Our Story

Bambana started with one frustration: buying a home in Uganda felt stressful, confusing and cold.

Our founder, after helping friends and family for years, realized people didn't just need listings. They needed someone who would listen, guide, and truly care.

So in 2020, Bambana was born. Named after the word for "banana" — a symbol of home growth and rootedness in Uganda.

We started with one goal: to make finding your home feel personal again. To combine global standards of luxury and professionalism with the warmth and trust of a Ugandan family.

Today, we've helped over 300 families move into homes they love, but our mission is still the same: we treat your home search like it's our own.

Hon. Nabilah Nagayi Ssempala
